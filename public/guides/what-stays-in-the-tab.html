What files stay in this tab actually means

FileTools Kit says work happens in this tab. That sentence is precise, not an air-gap. File bytes live in page memory. Hosting logs, fonts, card photos, and advertising services are a different channel.

Dropped files live as File or ArrayBuffer objects in the tab. pdf-lib, jsPDF, and Canvas read that memory. We do not operate an upload API.

FileTools Kit uses Google AdSense Auto ads. Google may use advertising and measurement technologies, including cookies or similar storage, subject to visitor choices and applicable consent requirements. Ad services do not receive a file upload from our servers because the tools do not operate an upload API.

This is not an air-gapped device and not a GDPR, CCPA, HIPAA, or COPPA certification.
