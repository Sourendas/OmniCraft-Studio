JPG, PNG, or WebP — which file to send

The format is a choice about the receiver, not a badge. FileTools Kit can re-encode PNG, JPEG, and WebP in the tab. It cannot make a blurry photo sharp by changing the extension.

A practical rule

Send JPEG for a camera photo, a receipt picture, or anything an old email form must accept. Send PNG for a screenshot, a logo, or a form where text must stay crisp. Send WebP when the site says it accepts WebP and you want a smaller photo than JPEG at a similar look. If the site does not list WebP, do not send it.

Converting JPEG to PNG usually makes a larger file and does not restore detail. Converting PNG to JPEG makes a screenshot smaller and can blur small text. Resize before you drop quality.

What this site will not convert

HEIC and AVIF are not a pipeline here. Export JPEG from the phone. Video and audio are not this tool. A PDF of images is a different job: JPG to PDF.
