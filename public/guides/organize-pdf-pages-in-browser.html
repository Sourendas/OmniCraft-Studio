Reorder or delete PDF pages in the browser

Organize PDF is for one file whose page order is wrong, or which has a blank page you do not want to send. You load the PDF in this tab, move or drop pages, and download a new file. The original on disk is not rewritten.

This is page order, not editing. You are not changing a paragraph. You are deciding which pages exist in the download and in what order.

  1. Open Organize PDF and add one PDF.
  2. Move pages until the list matches the packet you want. Remove pages you do not want in the download.
  3. Export and open the file. Count the pages before you email it.
  • Encrypted files often fail.
  • Deleting a page here does not redact words on the pages you keep.
  • Refreshing the tab clears the working copy.
