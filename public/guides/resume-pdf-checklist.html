Resume PDF checklist before you send

Last updated 10 October 2026 · Written by Souren Das

Most resume problems that cost interviews are not about wording. They are a wrong phone number, a leftover line of sample text, a file called document(3).pdf, or a certificate where the resume should be. This checklist takes about ten minutes and catches those. It is written for a PDF from Resume Builder but works for any resume. It is not career coaching and not a score from an employer system.

1. Open the file you will attach

Not the preview in the builder: the downloaded PDF, opened in a PDF reader or your browser. That is the file the recruiter sees. Check it on your phone too if you can, since many recruiters first open attachments on a phone.

2. No sample text left

The builder opens with a fictional engineer, Alex Mercer, who worked at Vanguard Cloud Systems and Apex Digital Labs and studied at the University of California, Berkeley. Use your reader's search (Ctrl+F or Cmd+F) for Alex, Mercer, Vanguard, Apex, Berkeley, and example.com. Any hit means a field was not replaced. Also search for "lorem" and "sample" if you started from any other template.

3. Contact details that reach you

  • The name matches the one on your certificates and the application form.
  • The email is one you check daily, and looks professional.
  • The phone number has the country code if you apply abroad (+91 for India) and is the one you answer.
  • LinkedIn or GitHub links open the right profile. Click them in the PDF.

4. Text, not a picture

Drag-select a sentence and copy it into a plain note. If the paste is clean, the text layer is real and a portal's parser can read it. The builder's PDF is real text. If you later print it, photograph the page, and rebuild a PDF from JPGs, you have thrown that away.

5. Dates, gaps, and consistency

Every role uses the same date format, such as Jun 2024 – Present. Current roles say Present, not an end date in the future. Titles match what an employer would confirm in a background check. If there is a gap longer than a few months, decide whether to explain it in one line; leaving it unexplained is fine too, but know your answer.

6. The overlap percentage is not a verdict

The builder's Keyword overlap checks 15 fixed software terms and nothing else. Use it only to notice a tool name you used and forgot to mention. Do not paste the job ad into the skills line. The percentage is local keyword overlap, not Naukri, Greenhouse, or Workday.

7. File name

The builder names the file from your name and layout, such as Priya_Sharma_classic.pdf. Rename it to something a recruiter can file, for example Priya-Sharma-Resume.pdf, or Priya-Sharma-Resume-Data-Analyst.pdf if you tailor versions. Avoid resume-final-v3.pdf.

8. Packet order, if you must send one

Merge certificates only if asked. If you merge with Merge PDF, put the resume first, open the merged file, and check that page 1 is still the resume. Do not attach Aadhaar, PAN, or passport scans "to be helpful"; send ID only when an employer asks, through their official channel.

9. Size and format

A text resume from the builder is typically small, well under most portal caps. If a portal rejects it for size, the cause is usually something merged in, such as a scanned certificate. Shrink scans before merging; see how to get a PDF under 1 MB or 200 KB. Do not password-protect a resume; portals and recruiters need to open it.

10. The email itself

Write a clear subject, such as "Application: Data Analyst – Priya Sharma". Put two or three lines in the body saying which role and why, and attach the PDF rather than a link to a drive folder. Send a test to yourself first and open the attachment from that email.

Common mistakes

  • Sending the preview screenshot instead of the PDF.
  • Leaving a sample bullet that mentions React 19 or Vite when you have never used them.
  • Attaching the wrong version when you keep several tailored copies.
  • A phone number missing a digit. Read it aloud.

Privacy

The builder runs in your tab and saves a draft only in your browser. Once you email the PDF, it is in the recruiter's systems; send only what they need.

FAQ

Should I send Word or PDF?

PDF, unless the employer asks for Word. A PDF looks the same everywhere.

One page or two?

One page for freshers and early-career roles. Two pages is normal with more experience.

Does this checklist guarantee the ATS will read my file?

No. It removes common avoidable problems. Every system parses differently.
