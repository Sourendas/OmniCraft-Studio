FileTools Kit · Privacy

Cookie & Storage Policy

Last updated: 3 September 2026

FileTools Kit does not set advertising or analytics cookies today. The AdSense script is not embedded. Until AdSense is enabled, no advertising cookie is set by FileTools Kit.

After Google approves the site and the official snippet is added, Google may set advertising and measurement cookies. Ads are third-party. File bytes are not uploaded to us and are not sent to advertisers from our servers.

Manage ads at adssettings.google.com. Google privacy: policies.google.com/privacy.

First-party keys may include UI prefs and ftk_ab_visitor, ftk_ab_assign, ftk_ab_events. Vercel, fonts, and Unsplash may set their own technical cookies.
