FileTools Kit · Privacy

Cookie & Storage Policy

Last updated: 4 September 2026

FileTools Kit does not set advertising or analytics cookies today. The AdSense script is not embedded. Until AdSense is enabled, no advertising cookie is set by FileTools Kit.

First-party storage

localStorage may hold UI preferences, optional A/B keys (ftk_ab_visitor, ftk_ab_assign, ftk_ab_events), and a Resume Builder draft (ftk_resume_v1). Clear site data for filetoolskit.com to delete them.

Hosting, fonts, and images

Vercel, Google Fonts, and Unsplash may set their own technical cookies when those assets load.

Advertising cookies — after approval only

After Google approves the site and the official snippet is added, Google may set advertising and measurement cookies. Ads are third-party. File bytes are not uploaded to us.

Manage ads at adssettings.google.com. Google privacy: policies.google.com/privacy.
