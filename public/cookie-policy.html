Cookie & Storage Policy

Last updated: 12 September 2026

A cookie is a small text file a site or a third party stores in your browser. Similar technologies include localStorage and pixels.

Advertising (Google AdSense)

FileTools Kit uses Google AdSense Auto ads. The publisher script is on this site (ca-pub-4409273905876536). Google may use advertising and measurement cookies or similar technologies, including personalized ads where permitted. FileTools Kit does not control Google’s advertising technologies.

EEA/UK/Switzerland visitors may see a Google Privacy & messaging consent experience. US-state visitors may see a Google US-state privacy notice when that message is published in AdSense.

Manage personalized ads at adssettings.google.com. Google privacy: policies.google.com/privacy. Partner sites: how Google uses data on partner sites.

File bytes opened in a tool are not uploaded to FileTools Kit and are not sent to advertisers from our servers.

Visit analytics

Vercel Web Analytics is cookie-less per Vercel. It does not receive file contents. Google Analytics is not installed.

First-party storage

localStorage may hold UI preferences, optional A/B keys (ftk_ab_*), and a Resume Builder draft (ftk_resume_v1). Clear site data for filetoolskit.com to delete them.

Hosting, fonts, and images

Vercel, Google Fonts, and Unsplash may set their own technical cookies.

How to control storage

Use browser cookie settings, Google Ads Settings, and any regional consent message Google shows.
