Cookie & Storage Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

A cookie is a small text file a website or a third party stores in your browser. Similar technologies include localStorage and tracking pixels. This page lists what FileTools Kit and its providers use. Read it with the privacy policy.

Advertising cookies (Google AdSense)

FileTools Kit shows ads from Google AdSense (ca-pub-4409273905876536). Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's advertising cookies let it and its partners serve ads based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet. Google and other certified ad networks also use cookies to measure ads and limit how often you see the same one. FileTools Kit does not control those cookies.

To opt out of personalized advertising, visit Google Ads Settings. To opt out of some other vendors' cookies for personalized advertising, visit www.aboutads.info or, in the EU and UK, www.youronlinechoices.eu. How Google uses data on partner sites: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Visitors in the European Economic Area, the UK, and Switzerland are asked for consent through Google's consent message before advertising cookies are used for personalized ads.

Visit analytics

Vercel Web Analytics counts page views. Vercel documents it as working without cookies. It does not receive file contents. Google Analytics is not installed.

Storage set by FileTools Kit

FileTools Kit itself sets no cookies. It may keep these keys in localStorage on your device:

  • ftk_ab_visitor, ftk_ab_assign, ftk_ab_events: a random ID, which page variant you saw, and a short local event log for layout tests. They are not sent to us.
  • ftk_resume_v1: your Resume Builder draft, so a reload does not lose it.

Hosting and badges

Vercel serves the site and may use strictly necessary technical cookies. When the app is loaded, three directory badge images appear at the very bottom of the page, below the site footer. They load from tools.launchllama.co, directree.io and sumodir.com.

How to control cookies and storage

Use your browser's settings to block or delete cookies, or clear site data for filetoolskit.com to remove the localStorage keys. Use the links above to manage personalized ads. Blocking cookies does not stop the tools from working.
