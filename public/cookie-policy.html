Cookie & Storage Policy

Last updated: 12 September 2026

FileTools Kit uses Google AdSense Auto ads. Google may use advertising and measurement cookies, subject to visitor choices.

Visit analytics

Vercel Web Analytics is cookie-less per Vercel. It does not receive file contents. Google Analytics is not installed.

First-party storage

localStorage may hold UI preferences, optional A/B keys, and a Resume Builder draft. Clear site data to delete them.

Hosting, fonts, and images

Vercel, Google Fonts, and Unsplash may set their own technical cookies.

Advertising

Google AdSense may set advertising cookies when ads are served. EEA/UK/Switzerland visitors may see Google’s European consent message. US-state visitors may see Google’s US state regulations message when that message is published in AdSense.

Manage ads at adssettings.google.com. Google privacy: policies.google.com/privacy.
