FileTools Kit · Privacy

Cookie & Storage Policy

Last updated: 10 September 2026

FileTools Kit uses Google AdSense Auto ads. Google may use advertising and measurement technologies, including cookies or similar storage, subject to visitor choices and applicable consent requirements.

Visit analytics

Vercel Web Analytics is enabled and documented by Vercel as cookie-less. It records page path and coarse visit metadata so we can see which tools are opened. It does not receive file contents. Google Analytics is not installed.

First-party storage

localStorage may hold UI preferences, optional A/B keys (ftk_ab_visitor, ftk_ab_assign, ftk_ab_events), and a Resume Builder draft (ftk_resume_v1). Clear site data for filetoolskit.com to delete them.

Hosting, fonts, and images

Vercel, Google Fonts, and Unsplash may set their own technical cookies when those assets load.

Advertising

Google AdSense may set or access advertising and measurement cookies or similar technologies when ads are served. Ad serving and the technologies used are controlled by Google. Where required, FileTools Kit provides the applicable consent choices through Google's consent-management flow.

Manage Google advertising preferences at adssettings.google.com. Google privacy: policies.google.com/privacy.
