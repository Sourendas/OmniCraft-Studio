Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 September 2026

This policy describes how FileTools Kit (filetoolskit.com) handles information. It is a description of practice, not a certification of GDPR, CCPA, HIPAA, DPDP, or COPPA compliance.

1. Who we are

Operated by Souren Das, Bengaluru, Karnataka, India. Privacy: privacy@filetoolskit.com. Support: support@filetoolskit.com.

2. What the tools do not send us

The 12 utilities run in your browser with pdf-lib, jsPDF, qrcode, mammoth, Canvas, and Web Crypto. We do not operate an upload API that receives your PDFs, images, resume text, or other files you open in a tool. Closing the tab discards that working copy unless you downloaded it or a localStorage draft remains on the device.

That is not an air-gapped device. Browser extensions you installed can read a page.

3. What the website host may see

Hosted on Vercel. Page requests may include IP address, user agent, URL, referrer, and timestamps. Those logs are not a copy of a PDF you opened in a tool.

4. Visit analytics (Vercel Web Analytics)

We use Vercel Web Analytics to count page views and which routes are opened. Vercel documents it as cookie-less. Typical fields: path, referrer host, country, device, OS, browser. It does not receive file contents. We do not use Google Analytics or Meta Pixel today. Vercel privacy: vercel.com/legal/privacy-policy.

5. Third-party content

Tool-card photos may load from Unsplash. Google Fonts may load Outfit. Contact opens a mailto draft. We do not run a ticket database.

6. Advertising (Google AdSense)

FileTools Kit uses Google AdSense Auto ads. Google may display advertisements and use cookies, local storage, or similar technologies for advertising and measurement, subject to the choices available to visitors and applicable consent requirements. Ad placement and ad serving are controlled by Google.

Manage Google advertising preferences at Ads Settings. Google privacy: policies.google.com/privacy. Partner sites: how Google uses data on partner sites.

FileTools Kit does not sell file contents to advertisers. We do not operate an upload API for the files processed by the tools.

7. Local storage

UI prefs, optional A/B keys (ftk_ab_*), and resume draft ftk_resume_v1 may stay on this device. Clear site data to delete them.

8. Children

Not directed at children under 13.

9. International visitors

Operator in India. Hosting, analytics, advertising, and related services may process requests in other regions. We do not claim GDPR/CCPA/DPDP certification. Email privacy@filetoolskit.com about mailbox data. We cannot delete a PDF that never left your tab.

10. Retention and security

We do not keep tool files because we do not receive them. HTTPS protects the site in transit. Tools do not encrypt exported PDFs.

11. Requests and changes

Email privacy@filetoolskit.com. We respond from Bengaluru. The live page is the current version.
