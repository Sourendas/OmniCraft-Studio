Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

This policy describes how FileTools Kit (www.filetoolskit.com) handles information. It describes what the site actually does. It is not a certification of GDPR, CCPA, DPDP, or any other law.

1. Who runs this site

FileTools Kit is built and operated by Souren Das in Bengaluru, Karnataka, India. Write to support@filetoolskit.com for product questions and privacy requests.

2. Files you open in a tool

The tools run in your browser with libraries such as pdf-lib, PDF.js, jsPDF, qrcode, mammoth, the Canvas API, and Web Crypto. FileTools Kit does not operate an upload API that receives your PDFs, images, resume text, or other files. The working copy lives in the browser tab and is discarded when you close or reload it, unless you download the result or the Resume Builder keeps a draft on your device (section 7).

3. Hosting logs

The site is hosted on Vercel. Like any web host, Vercel receives ordinary request data when a page loads: IP address, browser user agent, the URL requested, referrer, and time. Those logs record that a page was loaded. They do not contain the files you open in a tool. Vercel's privacy policy: vercel.com/legal/privacy-policy.

4. Visit analytics (Vercel Web Analytics)

We use Vercel Web Analytics to count page views and see which pages people open. Vercel documents it as working without cookies. It records the page path, referrer, approximate country, and device, browser, and operating system type. It never receives file contents. Google Analytics and Meta Pixel are not installed.

5. Advertising (Google AdSense)

FileTools Kit shows ads served by Google AdSense (publisher ID pub-4409273905876536, listed in ads.txt). Ads are how the tools stay free.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising at www.aboutads.info, or, in the EU and UK, at www.youronlinechoices.eu.
  • Other ad technology partners that Google works with may also serve or measure ads through AdSense and may use their own cookies. Google publishes the list at support.google.com/admanager/answer/9012903.

How Google uses information from sites that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

Visitors in the European Economic Area, the UK, and Switzerland are asked for consent through Google's consent message before personalized ads are shown. Where consent is not given, Google may show non-personalized or limited ads. You can change your choice later from the privacy link the consent message adds to the page, or by clearing cookies for this site.

Ads never receive your files from us, because your files are not uploaded to us. FileTools Kit does not sell personal information.

6. Other third-party content

When the app is loaded (for example on the home page and tool pages), three directory badges appear at the very bottom of the page, below the site footer. Their images load from tools.launchllama.co, directree.io and sumodir.com, so those services see an ordinary image request. The contact links open your own email app. No other third-party scripts are loaded.

7. Storage on your device

The site may keep a few keys in your browser's localStorage: layout test keys that start with ftk_ab_ (a random ID, which page variant you saw, and a short local event log) and the Resume Builder draft ftk_resume_v1. They stay on your device and are not sent to us. Clear site data for filetoolskit.com in your browser to delete them. More detail is in the cookie policy.

8. Children

The site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

9. International visitors

The operator is in India. Vercel and Google may process request data in other countries, including the United States.

10. Email you send us

If you email us, we keep the message and your address in our mailbox to answer you. Ask at support@filetoolskit.com and we will delete the thread.

11. Changes

When this policy changes, the date at the top changes. The page you are reading is the current version.
